## How to Use

### Playing Songs
1. Click on any category card
2. See the playlist of songs
3. Click "Audio" or "Video" button next to each song
4. Player overlay opens with video/audio

### Using Hymnbook
1. Click on "📖 Hymnbook" category
2. View hymn with number, title, author, and lyrics
3. Use "← Previous" and "Next →" buttons to navigate
4. Smooth page-flip animation on each transition
