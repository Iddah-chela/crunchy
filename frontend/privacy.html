Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2025

Welcome to HolyVerse. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how HolyVerse ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By creating an account or using HolyVerse, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our Service:

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Username and password (stored in hashed form only)
  • Profile Information: Optional birthday (used for age verification and content filtering), profile picture
  • User-Generated Content: Posts, comments, messages, Bible notes, highlights, and any media you upload
  • Communication Data: Messages you send through our private messaging system, feedback and support inquiries
  • Payment Information: Donation transaction metadata (amount, date, payment method type). We do not store full credit card numbers or sensitive payment credentials

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Features used, pages visited, time spent on the app
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: Session cookies for authentication, preference cookies for saved settings
  • Local Storage Data: Bible content cached via IndexedDB for offline access, UI preferences stored in localStorage (lastBook, lastChapter, notes, highlights, and other app state)

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Authentication Providers: If you sign in using third-party services in the future, we may receive basic profile information from those providers
  • Payment Processors: Transaction confirmation and status from Paystack, Equity Bank, or other payment providers

Important: While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please do not share highly sensitive personal information in public posts or private messages.

11. Children's Privacy & Safety

HolyVerse is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

Age-Based Safety Features

  • Content Filtering: We collect birthdate information to calculate users' ages and apply appropriate content filters to ensure younger users do not see sensitive or mature content.
  • Age-Restricted Features (16+):
    • Private chat and messaging
    • Friend requests and friend management
    • Community posting and commenting
    • Testimony submissions
    Users under 16 can still read all content but cannot post or send private messages. This ensures a safe environment for all users.
  • Server-Side Content Moderation: All user-generated content is automatically scanned for inappropriate language, personal information (phone numbers, emails), and external links before being posted.
  • Report System: Users can report inappropriate content or behavior. All reports are reviewed by our moderation team.
  • Friend Request System: Users can only send private messages to accepted friends, not random strangers.

Parental Rights: If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has created an account without your consent, please contact us at holyverse12@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action.
